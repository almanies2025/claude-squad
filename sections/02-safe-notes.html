Real Talk: A SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) is Y Combinator's lightweight alternative to a convertible note — it is not debt, has no interest rate, no maturity date, and no repayment obligation. The founder wins because there's no countdown clock. The investor wins because they get either a discount or a valuation cap — whichever gives them more shares. The math contest: cap beats discount when the round is at a high enough valuation that the cap-price is below the discounted round price.
SAFE conversion price = MIN(Cap / Pre-money shares, Round price × (1−Discount))  |  Shares received = SAFE amount / Conversion price  |  Discount price = Round price × (1−d) ; Cap price = Cap / Pre-money shares outstanding
WORKED EXAMPLE — SAFE: Cap vs Discount Showdown
Given: SAFE amount = $200,000  |  Valuation cap = $3,000,000  |  Discount = 20%  |  Series A pre-money = $8,000,000  |  Existing shares outstanding = 4,000,000 (founders)  |  New option pool = 1,000,000 shares (carved out of pre-money)
STEP 1 — Compute round price per share (Series A)
Total shares post-option-pool = 4,000,000 + 1,000,000 = 5,000,000
Round price = Pre-money / Total shares
Round price = $8,000,000 / 5,000,000 = $1.60/share
Intuition: the option pool is carved out of pre-money, so it increases the share count but not the dollar pre-money.

STEP 2 — Compute the two SAFE conversion prices
Path A — Discount method:
Discount price = Round price × (1−Discount)
Discount price = $1.60 × 0.80 = $1.28/share

Path B — Cap method:
Cap price = Cap / Pre-money shares
Cap price = $3,000,000 / 4,000,000 = $0.75/share
Intuition: the $3M cap on a company with 4M pre-money shares means the SAFE investor gets to pretend they bought at $0.75/share — way cheaper than the $1.60 Series A price.

STEP 3 — Pick the LOWER price (better for the SAFE investor)
MIN($1.28, $0.75) = $0.75/share — Cap wins
Intuition: the cap kicks in because the $3M cap implies a valuation far below the $8M Series A pre-money.

STEP 4 — Compute SAFE shares received
Shares = SAFE amount / Conversion price
Shares = $200,000 / $0.75 = 266,667 shares

STEP 5 — Verify dilution impact
Total shares after SAFE conversion = 5,000,000 + 266,667 = 5,266,667
SAFE ownership% = 266,667 / 5,266,667
SAFE ownership = 5.06%
Compare: if discount had won, shares = $200K/$1.28 = 156,250 shares = 2.97% — the cap earns 2.1 percentage points more equity.

ANSWERS:
SAFE conversion price = $0.75/share (cap wins over 20% discount)
SAFE shares received = 266,667 shares
SAFE ownership (post-conversion) = 5.06%
Key Insight: The SAFE cap is anchored to pre-money shares (excluding the new option pool). A $3M cap on 4M founder shares = $0.75/share equivalent. The Series A investor pays $1.60/share. The SAFE investor saves $0.85/share — this is the economic benefit of the cap.
Common Mistake: Students sometimes divide the cap by post-option-pool shares. Wrong! SAFE caps always reference pre-money shares at the time of conversion — the option pool hasn't been issued yet from the SAFE's perspective.
