## Minor Observations

1. **Tender pipeline breadth (Opportunities, Partial):** The analysis focuses heavily on Phosphate Round B (USD 5-10B) as the anchor opportunity. A broader enumeration of expected tenders by commodity (gold, phosphate, copper, industrial minerals) and approximate timing would strengthen this section. However, the analysis correctly does not over-promise specific non-public tender information.

2. **Etimad registration complexity:** The analysis could benefit from noting typical timeline and documentation requirements for foreign supplier registration on Etimad. However, recommending retention of Saudi legal counsel (Section 3.1) appropriately delegates this detail.

3. **MOMRA classification grades:** The analysis references "Category 1-5, Grade A-D" but does not specify which classification level would be required for the target mining construction scope. This is appropriately deferred to legal counsel given the scope uncertainty.
