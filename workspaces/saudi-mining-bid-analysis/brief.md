# Brief: Saudi Mining Construction Bid — Strategic Analysis

## Client

Chinese mining company operating in Saudi Arabia. Manager is aware of market presence. A personal connection exists within the Saudi Ministry of Mining.

## Objective

Provide a comprehensive analysis for the company, which **already holds an EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) contract with the Saudi government for a mining construction project**. The analysis should cover: legal compliance risks (including the MIMR contact), EPC contract economics and IRR, and strategic recommendations for maximizing the value of the existing contract while preparing for future market opportunities.

## Deliverables Requested

### 1. Opportunities

- How the Ministry connection can be leveraged **legally and ethically**
- Early intelligence on upcoming tenders
- Understanding evaluation criteria
- Ensuring compliance with local regulations

### 2. Risks

- Legal risks: Saudi Arabia's Anti-Bribery Law, Saudi Vision 2030 regulations, China's overseas anti-corruption rules
- Reputational risks from using government contact
- Operational risks of influence or accessing tender information

### 3. Strategic Recommendations

- Registering on Etimad (government procurement platform)
- Obtaining necessary classifications from Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs
- Forming local joint ventures if required
- Transparent communication through official channels

### 4. Competitive Landscape

- Local competitors (e.g., Saudi Binladin Group)
- International firms (e.g., Bechtel)
- Other Chinese players in Saudi mining construction

### 5. Practical Next Steps

- Setting up legitimate meetings via the relationship
- Requesting pre-qualification documents
- Gaining non-confidential guidance without crossing ethical/legal lines

## Output Format

Bullet points with clear recommendations for manager.

## Constraints

- All recommendations must be legally and ethically defensible
- No facilitation payments or corrupt practices
- Must comply with both Saudi Arabian and Chinese (overseas) regulations
