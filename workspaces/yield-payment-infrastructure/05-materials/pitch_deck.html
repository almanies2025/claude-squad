The Arithmetic

$5.5–7T in checking deposits generates $220–280B/year in float income at 4%. Banks pay customers 0.1–0.5%. The spread is pure profit.

The Validation

Stablecoins (USDC + USDT, $150B+ AUM) prove customers will shift for yield. They earn issuers ~$6–7B/year in seigniorage.

Neobanks with 20M+ users offer 0% yield. They have the users — but not the regulatory path. That gap is FloatYield's market.

Selected: Raise Capital Upfront, Run Parallel Tracks

ILC charter needs $4–8M. Previous flaw: capital raised from projected CUSO fees — but CUSO takes 12–18 months (realistic), not 6–12. Fee revenue delayed → runway depleted → ILC lapses → model collapses.

Fix: Raise $2M seed (covers ILC application + 12-month runway); file ILC immediately; run Sponsor Bank + CUSO in parallel.

ILC Denial Contingency (29% denial rate)

If ILC denied at month 18: Sponsor Bank partnership already running, CUSO partnerships established. Business survives. ILC becomes a future filing with larger capital base from CUSO revenue.

ILC denial is a runway event, not a business-ending event.

Year 1–2: Parallel Tracks

Sponsor Bank → fee revenue. CUSO → reduce bank dependency. ILC application → filed and in review.

Year 3+: ILC or CUSO Independence

ILC approved → full strategic independence. ILC denied → CUSO network provides bank-independent path.

Risk Register

Critical Risks

  • ● Reconciliation divergence: ~$900K/yr on $1B book
  • ● CUSO partnership takes >18 months (vs. 6-12 projected)
  • ● ILC charter denied (~29% denial rate, 2024-2026)
  • ● Sponsor Bank terminates at Year 2–3
  • ● Interest rate decline (<3.5% Treasury)

Mitigations

  • Raise $2M seed upfront (not from future fees)
  • Buffer CUSO timeline by 6 months
  • 3-year min Sponsor Bank contract
  • ILC denial contingency explicitly planned
  • Reconciliation reserve funded from Year 1
